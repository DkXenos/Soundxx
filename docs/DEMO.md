# Demo script (about 8 minutes)

For showing Soundxx to someone: a lecturer, a classmate, an examiner. You need the MacBook, the
Buds, and two people who can talk (you and the person watching is enough). Do steps 1–2 before
they arrive.

## Before

1. Open Soundxx and record about two minutes in a real lecture or a quiet room with two people
   talking, so there's a processed session with notes to show. Name yourself with "Remember this
   voice" while it records.
2. Close Soundxx and open it again, so the start is fresh.

## 1. Before class (1 minute)

- Open Soundxx. Point at the moving mic meter and the **Before you start** panel: it says in plain
  words what would go wrong (Buds not connected, lid closed, low disk, mic permission) and how to fix
  it.
- Take the Buds out of the case and watch "Play into" pick them up. Mention that the MacBook
  speakers can't be chosen, and why (the mic would hear them and howl).

## 2. In class (3 minutes)

- Type a title and press **Start recording**. Hand the visitor one Bud.
- Talk normally. The visitor hears you, denoised, about a fifth of a second late. Drop a pen or
  rustle paper: the denoiser takes most of it away.
- Your name appears in **Speakers** (you saved your voice), the visitor's appears as "Speaker N"
  after a few seconds of talking. The transcript fills in on the right, labelled.
- Click the visitor's name: Focus mode. Now they're at full volume and you're 20 dB down in the Bud.
  Click **Everyone** to go back.
- Press **Pause**, say something, **Resume**: the paused part isn't in the recording or transcript.
- **Stop recording**.

## 3. After class (3 minutes)

- The session opens and the careful transcript starts in the background (watch the progress in the
  top bar). While it runs, open the session you prepared.
- Click a chapter in the notes: the recording jumps there and the line being played is highlighted.
- Fix something live: rename "Mahasiswa 1" through **Change**, correct a misheard word with **Edit**,
  then **Undo**.
- Point out the wavy underlines (lines the model wasn't sure about) and the privacy line under the
  notes: only text went to Claude.
- **Show in Finder**: everything is ordinary files (Markdown, WAV, JSON) in one folder per lecture.

## If something goes wrong on stage

- Buds don't connect: choose "No earbuds"; recording and transcript work the same.
- No internet: notes say "waiting for an internet connection"; everything else works.
- Something crashes: reopen Soundxx and show **Recover**. Nothing recorded is lost; that is
  itself a good demonstration.
